Czech Rocket Society

Czech Rocket Society is an independent society for high school and university students who are interested in cosmonautics and rockets. We want to give them an opportunity to put their studies into practice, but at the same time to develop themselves, to test and experiment. At the same time, we want to take a comprehensive approach to rockets and cosmonautics, which is why, for example, we are interested in the legality of rockets, we participate in educational events and also organize the Czech Rocket Challenge.

Join us Visit czechrockets.com

Basic approximate information and concept of the competition

The Czech Rocket Challenge is a team competition designed for high school and university students. In this challenge, teams of students* design, test, build and finally launch their rocket. Not only the height reached, but also the advancedness and complexity of the rocket, the sophistication of the report, etc. will be evaluated.

What does this mean? We will give you the engine, but the rest is up to you! Focus on an effective structure that can carry the largest water load, and make sure the rocket lands safely. Join this year’s Czech Rocket Challenge with your team.

* The team must have at least one adult member. In the case of a high school team, this can be a teacher/mentor.

Advanced

Are you more experienced rocketeers or do you want a real challenge? Your task is to design a PocketQube (1P) satellite and successfully release it from the rocket at apogee. You will be evaluated for the precision of your work.
